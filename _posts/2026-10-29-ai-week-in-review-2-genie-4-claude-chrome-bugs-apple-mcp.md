The second batch is heavier on products and research. Here is what stood out.

## Google DeepMind: Genie 4

- **Genie 4** generates photorealistic, interactive worlds in real time, and for the first time generates ambient sound, music and character dialogue natively alongside the visuals.
- You can change the weather, move the camera or add people with plain text while the simulation runs.
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash** acts as an in-the-loop agent that breaks goals into steps and checks frames for progress.
- It is a research preview for trusted testers for now, with Google AI Ultra subscribers (Project Genie) and early Gemini API / AI Studio partners next.

## Anthropic: bug hunting and government deals

- Anthropic's Frontier Red Team used an unreleased internal model to build **five** critical attack chains. The Chrome work alone led to **62 CVEs**, patched as of Chrome 156. Others include a Safari same-origin bypass and a zero-click root exploit in the Linux kernel.
- A new GSA agreement gives federal, state, local and tribal governments **40% off** Claude Enterprise, Claude for Government and the Claude API, with no minimum spend. Anthropic estimates **$1 billion** in savings; about **500** US government organizations use Claude.

## Microsoft: MAI Image 2.7

- The preview model debuted at **No. 2** on Artificial Analysis's image-editing leaderboard with **1193 Elo**, behind GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst (1199) and ahead of GPT Image 2.5 Flare (1182). It is **No. 3** on Arena's edit board.
- It beats MAI Image 2.6 on all 10 use cases and 7 editing actions, leading scene-and-style edits by **24 Elo**. No text-to-image or API yet.

## Chips and platforms

- **OpenAI** plans to buy Google TPUs directly, The Information reports. Neither company has confirmed.
- **OpenAI and Oracle** announced Oracle AI Workspace, letting employees use approved Oracle data and workflows from inside ChatGPT.
- **Nvidia and Oracle** are scaling Vera Rubin NVL72 superclusters on OCI to multi-gigawatt scale. Oracle will also offer Google TPUs on OCI from 2027.
- **Apple** is adding assistants built on Apple models and OpenAI to Keynote, Pages and Numbers (beta, US English) in a November update, plus **MCP** support on macOS 27 so outside agents can read and edit documents.

## Quick hits

- **SpaceXSI** described how its sales team splits work across three Grok Bots: Account Intel, Quote Bot and Deal Bot, with humans signing off on customer-facing decisions.
- **Ant Group** launched LingGuang 2.0, which the company says can keep following up on tasks after you leave the chat.
- **Liquid AI** released LFM3, six open-weight models of about 2B parameters each for text, vision, audio, omni and decision tasks, fine-tunable on a single GPU.
- **Axiom** published a complete Lean 4 formalization of the Classification of Finite Simple Groups, which it says runs to about **43.1 million lines**.

## Takeaway

World models are getting sound and real-time control, AI bug hunting is operating at scale, and MCP is quietly becoming the default way agents plug into apps.
