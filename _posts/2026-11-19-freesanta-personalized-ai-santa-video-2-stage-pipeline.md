## Where AI holiday video generators fall short, honestly:

- **Long spoken name lists in a single pass**: Asking a raw text-to-video prompt to pronounce three different children's names in a 15-second monologue without dedicated lip-sync dubbing often drifts out of sync after second five. Keep visual greeting clips to punchy **5-to-8-second warm reveals** and layer custom voiceovers cleanly.
- **Tiny cursive text on moving ribbons**: While flat parchment scrolls and wooden signs hold lettering well when anchored from a `Seedream 5 Pro` start frame, waving a ribbon rapidly across the lens blurs small script.
- **Consent and family photo privacy**: When uploading family portraits for holiday relighting in `Nano Banana 2.1`, only use photos you have explicit permission to edit.
